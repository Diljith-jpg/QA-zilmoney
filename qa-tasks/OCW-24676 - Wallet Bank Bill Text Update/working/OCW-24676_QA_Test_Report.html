Task Overview

The task updates the help text on three areas so it describes what each action actually does. On the feature build, the Transfer tooltips on Wallets and Bank Accounts, the Import Bills tooltip on Bills and the wallet placeholder in Recharge My Credits were checked in Version 2.0 against the texts QA supplied. All the checked texts are updated. Two wording differences and one scope question are listed under Open Points. The classic (V3.5) UI was also looked at and still shows the old text.

Issue / Requirement Description

  • Old texts: Wallet Transfer tooltip "Transfer the fund from your wallet"; Bank Account Transfer tooltip "Money moves between your account"; Bills import text "Bulk import bills from a file or external source"; Recharge wallet field "From Wallet Account".
  • Requirement (texts supplied by QA): Transfer tooltip "Transfer the funds from your wallets or accounts."; Import Bills tooltip "Import bills from your connected third-party apps ."; Recharge My Credits placeholder "Select Wallet".
  • Ticket note: the ticket says import supports an external CSV file "not … importing from connected third-party apps". This conflicts with the new tooltip (see Open Points).

Updates / Changes Implemented

  • Wallets (All Wallets) "Transfer Funds" and Bank Accounts (All Accounts) "Transfer" tooltips now read "Transfer the funds from your wallets or accounts".
  • Single-wallet and single-bank-account "Transfer" tooltips now read "Transfer money to your wallets or accounts".
  • Bills "Import Bills" tooltip now reads "Import bills from your connected third-party apps".
  • Recharge My Credits: the field label is "From Wallet" and the placeholder is "Select Wallet".

Testing & Verification

  • Verified that hovering "Transfer Funds" on All Wallets shows "Transfer the funds from your wallets or accounts". The exact string was confirmed in the DOM.
  • Verified that the single-wallet "Transfer" tooltip shows "Transfer money to your wallets or accounts".
  • Confirmed that Recharge My Credits, opened from All Wallets, shows the label "From Wallet" with the placeholder "Select Wallet". From a single wallet the wallet is prefilled; after clearing it, the placeholder is "Select Wallet".

Bank Accounts (Version 2.0)

  • Confirmed that the console showed no errors on the Bills page.

Classic UI (V3.5) — observation

Open Points

  • Detail views (TC02, TC06) say "Transfer money to your wallets or accounts", while the list views say "Transfer the funds from your wallets or accounts". Should all four match?
  • The supplied texts have a trailing "." (and "apps ." has a space before it). The UI shows neither. This is assumed fine; please confirm.
  • The ticket note says import does not support connected third-party apps, but the new tooltip says it does, and the dialog shows connected apps. Please confirm with the reporter that the tooltip wording is approved.
  • The classic (V3.5) UI still shows the old Transfer tooltip and the old "From Wallet Account" placeholder. Is the change meant for Version 2.0 only?
