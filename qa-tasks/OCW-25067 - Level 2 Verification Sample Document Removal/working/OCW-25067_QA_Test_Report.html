Task Overview

The Level 2 business verification step showed a generic "Sample formation document" section next to the state-specific formation document examples. This could confuse users and lead to wrong uploads. The build removes the generic section and its PDF and keeps the state-wise examples. QA tested the Level 2 business verification step on staging: the generic sample no longer appears, "View examples" still shows the state-specific examples, and document upload and submission work as before.

Issue / Requirement Description

  • Reported: Level 2 business verification showed both a generic sample formation document ("Sample formation document – Reference PDF to guide your upload") and state-specific examples.
  • Requirement: remove the outdated "Sample formation document" section and its downloadable reference PDF.
  • Requirement: keep the state-specific formation document examples and the "View examples" option.
  • Constraint: document upload and submission must not change, and the removal must apply wherever the outdated sample appeared in the Level 2 workflow.

Updates / Changes Implemented

  • The "Sample formation document – Reference PDF to guide your upload" section is no longer shown in Level 2 business verification.
  • The download option for the generic reference PDF is gone.
  • The state-specific formation document examples are still available through "View examples".

Testing & Verification

Outdated sample removed

  • Verified that the "Sample formation document" section is no longer shown in the Level 2 business verification step.
  • Verified that the generic reference PDF can no longer be downloaded from the step.
  • Confirmed that the outdated sample does not appear anywhere else in the Level 2 workflow.

State-specific examples retained

  • Verified that "View examples" is still shown and opens the state-specific formation document examples.

Upload and submission regression

  • Confirmed that the formation document can still be uploaded and that Level 2 verification can still be submitted as before.
  • Confirmed that the rest of the step's layout looks correct after the section was removed.
