# OCW-24638 — Bulk Payment Table - Payment Count and Total Amount Not Filtering

- Type: Task (Bug, P2) · Priority: Medium · Status at QA: In QA
- Assignee (dev): hari sankar · Reporter: Ansha Shirin (raised from ATS)
- Link: https://zilmoney.atlassian.net/browse/OCW-24638

## Summary
Payment Count and Total Amount on the Bulk Payment table do not reflect applied filters.

## Acceptance criteria
- Applying a filter correctly updates the displayed Payment Count.
- Applying a filter correctly updates the displayed Total Amount.
- Payment Count reflects the number of records visible after filtering.
- Total Amount reflects the sum of values for visible records after filtering.

- Tested page: V4 Bulk Payment Center → My Groups table (/v4/manage/bulk-payments/index).
